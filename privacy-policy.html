Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how MSRDC – Grievance Redressal Management System (GRMS) collects, uses, and protects data received through Meta (Facebook / Instagram) APIs.

1. Data We Collect

Our application may receive the following data via Meta Webhooks:

  • Public post mentions of our official account
  • Comments on public posts mentioning our account

2. Purpose of Data Collection

The collected data is used solely for the purpose of:

  • Identifying public grievances
  • Tracking and responding to grievances
  • Improving grievance redressal services

3. Data Retention

Data is stored only for the duration required to resolve the grievance. Once the issue is resolved, the associated data is deleted or anonymized.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share collected data with third parties. Data is accessed only by authorized personnel.

5. User Data Deletion

Users can request deletion of their data by sending an email to:

aipl@amnex.com

6. Contact Information

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
