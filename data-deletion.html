User Data Deletion Instructions

Users who wish to request deletion of data collected through MSRDC – Grievance Redressal Management System (GRMS) may follow the steps below.

How to Request Data Deletion

  1. Send an email to aipl@amnex.com
  2. Include your social media profile link or post reference
  3. Mention “Data Deletion Request” in the subject line

Deletion Timeline

Upon verification, the requested data will be deleted within 7 to 30 business days, unless required to be retained by law.

Contact

For any queries related to data deletion, contact:
aipl@amnex.com
